# Exploring at different scales

The personal map starts in its spatial presentation. Zooming changes the information shown without changing saved coordinates or group membership.

- **Groups:** separate colored containers with readable group names and exact visible chat/note counts. More documents give a group more area, with a readable minimum size for small groups. Ungrouped items remain discoverable in their own container. Connections between containers summarize actual branches and personal links. Untitled miniature nodes are omitted.
- **Titles:** manual zoom enlarges the containers and reveals titled cards inside every group once there is enough room. Titles shrink with their cards from 13px down to 8px, staying visible through 40% zoom; below that, the group summaries take over. Text wraps to the number of lines that fit, and small group headings drop their counts to leave space for document titles. Neighboring groups remain on the map; zooming does not select or focus a group. Selecting a card opens its details in the reader while preserving the surrounding groups.
- **Working:** zooming further gives titles more space. Unselected nodes do not reveal message bodies or metadata as a side effect of zooming.
- **Selection:** only the selected node has a preview. Reading, editing, and opening in split view remain explicit actions. Controls appear on hover, selection, or keyboard focus; secondary actions share one menu.

The overview packs group containers with clear gutters, even when the saved chat positions interleave. Clicking a group or its magnifying-glass-plus icon explicitly focuses it and fits its own readable cards inside a colored container. The persistent **All groups** button clears group focus and details, then frames the group overview. **Fit group** frames the current group's cards inside the available canvas. These actions never save new node coordinates. Existing group membership is flat; overlapping saved bounds do not imply a subgroup relationship.

When Jev assistance is enabled, the existing workspace analysis service categorizes group names and permitted member context. Groups with the same semantic category occupy neighboring overview slots. Categories are cached by account and evidence fingerprint; pan and zoom do not trigger model requests. Uncertain or unavailable judgments retain a stable fallback order. This arrangement does not change group membership.

Focused groups show only their own titled cards and relationships, without numbered markers or a separate member-list panel. Dense groups temporarily space cards into a grid; returning to **All groups** restores the container overview. On a canvas too small for every readable card or group container, users can pan to the remaining content. Selecting a card opens its reader without changing zoom.

Selecting a nearby node preserves the current zoom and only pans enough to reveal clipped content. Search, focus requests, and Center position the selected node in the usable canvas, leaving room for the details dock. The canvas uses `overflow: clip` to prevent browser focus scrolling from offsetting this camera. Resizing reveals the selection without resetting its zoom. Back and Forward restore recorded cameras, group focus, selection, and details.

All three maps support one-finger panning, two-finger touchscreen pinch, and keyboard controls on the canvas: arrows pan (Shift doubles the step), and +/− zoom. Two-finger trackpad scrolling pans through group labels and clickable edges as well as the background; edge clicks still open their source or connection details. Nodes and outlines track the camera together without a transform animation delay, and pure panning reuses the existing group-label layout. Home or 0 returns to all groups in personal and public maps, and fits the URL map. Text inputs and node buttons retain their normal keys. Personal and URL readers resize on desktop and collapse into a bottom sheet on narrow screens.

Personal and public connections use cubic Bézier curves, including group links and focused neighborhoods. Labels follow each curve's midpoint, and interactive hit areas follow the same path. Tree layouts retain their directional routing, with a gentle bow for aligned nodes.

The personal map combines its sidebar toggle, map switcher, history, **All groups**, search, and Explore into one compact toolbar. **Add** holds note and web-source creation. View options offer **Groups and documents**, **Documents and connections**, and the theme presentation. The documents view removes group outlines, labels, and collapsed-group summaries; document cards and their real connections remain. Its selection persists through zoom, search, reading, and history, and **All groups** explicitly returns to grouped browsing. Map mode omits the separate application title bar, and Related appears only when there are valid suggestions. Zoom reveals detail automatically, without a scale selector; the floating controls retain Arrange, zoom, Fit, and selection centering.

The documents view retains well-spaced saved positions when they fit at a readable scale. Dense or extremely distant saved positions use a temporary grid with the original connections. This arrangement does not overwrite saved coordinates. Cards shrink with the camera, including below the title readability limit, so they never pile up as a side effect of keeping text readable.

In the documents view, **Arrange** offers **Auto layout**, **Tree: left to right**, **Tree: top down**, and **Most connections**. Tree layouts follow the loaded branch and manual connections, placing disconnected trees beside one another without inventing edges. Cycles remain visible but do not force a cyclic hierarchy. Most connections centers and highlights the document with the most distinct neighbors; repeated, reversed, missing, and self-links do not inflate that count. Connected documents form surrounding rings, and disconnected documents remain reachable farther out. Ties follow the stable document order. The chosen layout and camera survive navigation history and reopening the map; Fit frames the chosen tree or returns to its central document. Layout choices never overwrite saved node positions.

**Focus connections** enters a local subgraph around the selected document or public topic. It includes both incoming and outgoing loaded connections, including manually linked personal documents, starting one step away. The focused node sits at the center of compact rings with readable cards; unrelated groups disappear. **Show more connections** includes another step, clicking a nearby node pivots the neighborhood, and **All nodes** returns to the complete document or topic view. The personal reader closes when entering focus to make room for scanning, and its explicit dock action remains available. Tree arrangements also work within this subgraph; the radial option becomes **Around focused node**. Focus, depth, layout, and camera are restored by navigation history. Very large neighborhoods remain pannable at a readable scale.

Quick search results open beneath the search field. Explore opens the existing workspace sidebar's **Explore** section, next to **Chats & notes**. Its bounded source list has independent scrolling. When the personal graph is rendered outside the application shell, Explore uses a closable popover. Public topic details use a resizable dock on desktop and a collapsible bottom sheet on narrow screens.

The Public map uses the same continuous group expansion, readable titles, and explicit click-to-focus behavior. Its distant view shows exploration neighborhoods, named for their starting topics. These are browsing paths, not inferred semantic categories. Shared topics count once across displayed neighborhoods, and connection edges come from the loaded public relationships. Expansion, saving, and returning to a saved personal node retain their existing behavior.

Public **Map view options** switches between **Groups and topics** and **Topics and connections**. The latter uses the same four Arrange choices as the personal document view, plus Fit, Center, and zoom. Relationship filters remain active in focused subgraphs; showing another neighborhood step uses loaded connections, while **Expand connections** fetches additional public relationships.

Verification covers usable-canvas centering with a dock, readable card layouts, group counts, label separation, shared/cyclic public neighborhoods, trackpad movement, and existing focus, source, history, and sidebar interactions. Interaction fixtures cover desktop and phone canvases; live browser checks also use the local app connected to production.
