MarginWeb clipper

A place for what you find.

Connect this browser to your Margin Chat Cloud Inbox.

  1. Open Margin Chat and choose Cloud Inbox → Connect extension.
  2. Create a capture key, then enter your website address and paste the key below.

Your key can save captures and check the connection. It cannot read your Inbox, notes, or chats. Captures are sent only when you save. Keys expire after 90 days and can be revoked in Margin Chat.
